title: "Laws for the AI-Recreated Dead: Learning from China's Precedent"
original_title_ja: "AI故人の法を考える：中国の先例から"
description: "Countries around the world are drafting AI laws, yet most of them say little about the dead, or treat them only through the commercial use of celebrity likenesses. China is an exception. Since 2025, its administrative, regulatory and judicial authorities have each named the dead explicitly and begun to give legal language to the AI recreation of the dead. This essay asks where China stands among the world's AI laws, and why it was able to go so far."
